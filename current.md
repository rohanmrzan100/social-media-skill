# POV: LAZY DAY — Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

**Shoot mode:** solo, phone on tripod (one friend optional for the mirror/reveal shots). Budget phone + clip-on mic for the voiceover. This one is an *at-home getting-ready* story, so it lives in the bedroom / mirror / wardrobe — don't force it outdoors.

**Audio bed:** pick ONE trending "lazy morning / getting-ready" sound that has a clear **beat drop**. Save the beat drop for the kurti reveal (Scene 5). VO recorded separately, layered over low music.

## Why this build works (quick audit)

- **Skip-rate defense:** cold open in bed, no logo intro — the *"this is me"* hook lands in the first second.
- **The one shareable line:** *"Lazy day ho bhandai ma naramro dekhina ni koslai man huncha ra?"* — the relatable truth women tag friends on.
- **Comment driver:** *"Tapai ko go-to dress ke ho? Comment garnus ta"* + the open loop *"tara sachhi bhannu parda.."* — leaves a gap people want to fill.
- **Kurti as vehicle:** product only appears as a soft resolution at Scene 5–6, tagged, never priced. ~80/20 held.
