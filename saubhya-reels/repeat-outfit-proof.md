# Saubhya Reel — "Repeat outfit = proof"

Seed idea: *re-wearing the same kurti isn't embarrassing — it's proof you chose
right.* One-true-line contrarian (hook-machine Frame 6 — her most proven frame),
anti-trend/worthiness message, fresh angle in place of the retired fit-line.
**Delivery = hybrid:** faceless open, face for the closing truth. Target ~28s.
Solo-shootable.

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–4s | **Faceless.** The same kurti coming off the hanger — clearly loved: soft creases, worn-in. | "Same kurti dosro choti lagaunu laj ko kura ho re. Kasle bhanyo?" | Repeating the same kurti is supposed to be embarrassing. Says who? |
| 4–12s | Getting-ready beats with that kurti: sleeve, dupatta, mirror glance (face out of frame). | "Photo ma euta choti aaisakyo bhane feri lagauna hudaina — yo rule kasle banayo? Kina manne?" | Once it's been in a photo, you can't wear it again — who made this rule? Why follow it? |
| 12–20s | Close pass over the fabric — the worn-in softness as the visual argument. | "Euta luga feri feri lagauna man lagnu — tyo ta proof ho. Tapaile sahi chunnu bhayo bhanne proof." | Wanting to wear something again and again — that's proof. Proof you chose right. |
| 20–28s | **CUT TO FACE.** Calm, wearing it, straight to lens. | "Trend ko lagi hoina, aafno lagi kinnus. Ani man parunjel lagaunus — garva sanga." | Buy for yourself, not the trend. And wear it as long as you love it — with pride. |

**On-screen text (≤2 cards):**
- 0–4s: `repeat outfit?` (small, lower third)
- 20–28s: `proof ho.` (clean, centered)

Record VO for 0–20s separately; speak the closing lines live on the face shot.

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** names a belief half the audience quietly carries + "kasle bhanyo?" — the Frame-6 double-duty open (scroll-stop + argument-starter).
- **Shareable line:** "Repeat garnu laj hoina — proof ho." Gets reposted to Stories as a stance.
- **Comment driver:** "kati choti lagaunu bhayo hola?" — confession/count prompt; the take itself invites agree/disagree.
- **Cuts:** merge beats 2–3 for a ~22s version.

## Checklist before posting
- The contrarian take targets a *rule/belief*, never the women who follow it.
- Not preachy: "kasle bhanyo?" stays playful-calm, not activist-angry.
- ≤30s; no price, no backstory, no "fit" line, ≤2 text cards.
