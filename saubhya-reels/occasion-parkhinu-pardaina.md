# Saubhya Reel — "Occasion parkhinu pardaina"

Seed idea: *we save our good clothes for a "special day" that never comes.*
One-true-line register — message/POV piece (message reels break her ceiling; this
is the fresh angle, NOT the retired fit-line). **Delivery = hybrid:** faceless
open, cut to face for the closing truth. Target ~32s. Solo-shootable.

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–4s | **Faceless.** Hand pushes hangers aside, stops at a clearly-loved kurti pushed to the wardrobe's far end. | "Hami naya lugalai occasion ko lagi bachayera rakhchau. Kina?" | We save our new clothes for an occasion. Why? |
| 4–11s | Kurti lifted out, tag still on / crease from hanging. | "Ramro kurti kinchau — ani 'yo ta special din ko lagi' bhanera almari ma jhundyaidinchau." | We buy a beautiful kurti — then hang it away, "this one's for a special day." |
| 11–18s | Kurti back on the rail; wardrobe half-closes. Small pause. | "Ani tyo special din aaudai aaudaina. Luga tehi jhundiyeko jhundiyai." | And that special day never quite comes. The kurti just keeps hanging. |
| 18–26s | **CUT TO FACE.** Calm, straight to lens, wearing a kurti — clearly an ordinary day at home. | "Mangalbar pani ta tapai kai din ho. Aafai lai ramro feel garauna occasion chahidaina." | Tuesday is your day too. You don't need an occasion to make yourself feel good. |
| 26–32s | Hold, then a soft look away; ordinary-day frame (chiya, desk, home). | "Aaja nai lagaunus. Aaja lai nai special banaunus." | Wear it today. Make today the special one. |

**On-screen text (≤2 cards):**
- 0–4s: `occasion kina parkhine?` (small, lower third)
- 26–32s: `aaja nai lagaunus.` (clean, centered)

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** mid-action wardrobe motion + a question that indicts a near-universal habit — instant self-recognition ("I do this").
- **Shareable line:** "Mangalbar pani ta tapai kai din ho." — forwards as a gentle push to a friend who hoards her good clothes.
- **Comment driver:** the caption's "tapai sanga pani cha, hoina?" — a self-tag confession prompt, soft.
- **Cuts:** merge beats 2–3 for a ~26s version.

## Checklist before posting
- Tone check: gentle truth, not preachy — no "you SHOULD"; it's an invitation, not a lecture.
- ≤34s; no price, no backstory, no "fit" line, ≤2 text cards.
- Contrarian beat stays about a *habit/feeling*, never a swipe at the viewer.
