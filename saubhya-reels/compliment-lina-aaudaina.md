# Saubhya Reel — "Compliment lina aaudaina"

Seed idea: *women don't know how to receive a compliment* — outfit, work, anything.
Values / one-true-line POV piece. Premium register. Kurti = vehicle, not subject.
**Delivery = Hybrid:** open faceless on b-roll, cut to face for the closing line,
end on a quiet frame. Target ~34s. Solo-shootable on one phone, window light.

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–4s | **Faceless.** Hands buttoning / smoothing the kurti collar, or a soft mirror glance (face out of frame). | "Kasaile compliment garyo bhane, hami kina tyo compliment lina sakdainau? Kina awkward hunchau?" | When someone compliments us, why can't we take it? Why do we get awkward? |
| 4–10s | **Faceless.** Mirror glance, dupatta adjust, eyes flick away. | "Ma ta haserai taardinchu. 'Thank you' bhanna ni awkward lagiraako hunchha." | Me, I just laugh it off. Even saying 'thank you' feels awkward. |
| 10–15s | **Faceless.** Kurti on hanger; slow fabric / detail close-up. | "Tara yo ma matrai ho jasto lagdaina. Hami dherai yestai chau, hoina ra?" | But I don't think it's just me. A lot of us are like this, aren't we? |
| 15–23s | **Faceless.** Tying hair, slipping the kurti on, calm getting-ready beat. | "Har kura ma kati effort dinchau. Tara prashansa aayo bhane kina garva sanga lina sakdainau — kina aafnai achievement lai downplay garchau?" | We put so much effort into everything. But when praise comes, why can't we take it with pride — why do we downplay our own achievements? |
| 23–30s | **CUT TO FACE.** Calm, straight into the lens, still. | "Outfit hos, kaam hos — arule ramro bhandyo bhane, lina ni ta siknu parchha ni." | Outfit or work — when someone appreciates it, you have to learn to receive it too. |
| 30–34s | Hold the look, then a soft glance down / gentle turn away. Quiet end frame. | "Aja kasaile compliment garyo bhane — awkward nabhai, proudly 'thank you' bhannu." | Today if someone compliments you — no awkwardness, just say 'thank you' proudly. |

**On-screen text (sparingly):**
- 0–4s: `kina awkward hunchau?` (small, lower third)
- 30–34s: `proudly — thank you.` (clean, centered)
- Nothing else. No emoji wall, no CTA sticker.
