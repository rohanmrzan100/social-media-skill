# Saubhya Reel — "Dashain ko naya luga"

Seed idea: *as kids, Dashain's new clothes were bought weeks early and we took them
out to look at every single day until the day. We still do.* Feel-before-face
register with one relatable line — nostalgia, not comedy. **Delivery = her on
camera, no talking, trending song, ≤16s** — the skeleton of @prasuna.clo's biggest
organic reel ever (784k, "Only Kurtha you need for your girls day out.", 21s,
wordless, on a song) — with a line every Nepali woman recognises instead of a
product line. (Saubhya's own play counts are ad-driven and were NOT used here.)

Timing: Dashain is next month. Shopping intent is climbing from now until the
week before Ghatasthapana. Post in the last 10 days of September.

She leads: the kurti, the song, and the exact wording of the childhood line are
hers to change. Everything below is a shape, not a script to obey.

---

## Why this one can go big (mechanism, not hope)

- **Format = the kurti niche's proven organic ceiling.** @prasuna.clo's #1 (784k)
  and #5 (191k) reels are both ~21s, wordless, her in the kurti on a trending song,
  one relatable line. This copies that shape 1:1 and is the cheapest thing to
  film. Don't talk in this one.
- **Share loop.** "Sano huda Dashain ko naya luga…" is a shared childhood, not a
  brand line. It gets sent to a sister, a cousin, a bahini with "yo hamro kura ho".
  Shares are the reach signal Instagram weights most.
- **Season.** Dashain is the single biggest new-clothes moment in Nepal. The
  audience is already thinking it; the reel just says it first.
- **Rewatch.** The payoff card ("Ahile pani.") lands on the beat at ~5s, then the
  reel is just her in the kurti. People loop a 15s reel; loops count as plays.
- **Premium stays intact.** No joke, no self-deprecation, no "comment 👇", no
  price. One quiet truth, one clean subject, fabric visible.

## THE SCRIPT (~15–16s)

| Time | Visual | Audio | On-screen text |
|------|--------|-------|----------------|
| 0–2s | Same plain wall as her winners. She lifts the kurti on its hanger into frame, still looking at it, not at camera. | Song intro (trending Nepali / Dashain-season audio — her pick). | Card 1: `Sano huda Dashain ko naya luga…` |
| 2–5s | She holds it against herself, looks down at it. No performing — just the look you give a new outfit. | Song building. | Card 2: `…lagaune din samma daily nikalera hernu parthyo.` |
| 5–6s | **Cut on the beat.** She is wearing it. Eyes closed, slight sway. | Beat drop. | Card 3: `Ahile pani.` |
| 6–13s | Her in the kurti. One slow turn or hair tuck. One close-up of the embroidery or sleeve (2s). | Song. | none |
| 13–16s | Stands still, looks at camera, calm. Hold 2s. End. | Song fades / cut. | small `Saubhya` watermark, as usual |

**Text style:** one clean serif, cream/white, small, lower-third or centred. No
emoji, no yellow, no bold shouting. Three cards max. The text is a whisper, not a
title.

**What she does NOT do:** talk, explain the kurti, name the fabric, show a
second option, point at a price, ask anything.

## Shot list (solo, one phone, tripod, window light) — ~20 minutes

Setup: phone on tripod at chest height, vertical, main lens, the plain wall she
always uses, daylight from the side. Same mark on the floor for every take.

1. **Clip A (5s, one take, 3 tries):** hold the kurti on the hanger just out of
   frame, lift it in, look at it, hold it against yourself. Don't look at the
   phone. Pick the take where the look is most natural.
2. **Clip B (10s, 3 tries):** change into it. Stand on the same mark. Eyes closed,
   sway for 2s, then one slow turn or hair tuck, then stand still and look at the
   camera for 2s. That's the whole take.
3. **Clip C (3s):** phone in hand, close on the embroidery or sleeve, slow drift.
   Optional — skip if the light is gone.

Edit (in the IG editor or CapCut, 10 minutes):
- Pick the audio first. Find the first strong beat between 4–7s. Cut A → B on it.
- Drop the 3 text cards at the times above. Card 3 lands exactly on the cut.
- Trim to ≤16s. Watch it muted once: still premium? Then post.

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** motion from frame zero (kurti lifting in), a face, and
  a text card that starts a sentence — the viewer stays to finish it.
- **The shareable line:** "Sano huda Dashain ko naya luga lagaune din samma daily
  nikalera hernu parthyo." Universal, specific, sends itself to a sister.
- **Conversation earned, not begged:** "Ahile pani." invites "sachai 😭" and people's
  own memory of the rule (some couldn't wear it before Dashami). No ask needed.
- **Cuts:** one real cut. Premium is pace.

## If it works

Repeat the shape once a week until Dashain with a different kurti and a different
childhood line (same wall, same song family, same three-card rhythm). A repeatable
format is worth more than one hit — it's what turns one hit into followers.

## Checklist before posting
- ≤16s. She never talks. No price. Three text cards, serif, no emoji.
- Trending audio, not original audio.
- Watch muted: reads premium? Watch with sound: cut lands on the beat?
- Caption has no hashtag wall.
