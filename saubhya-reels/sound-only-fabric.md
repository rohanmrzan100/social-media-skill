# Saubhya Reel — "Sound only" (fabric ASMR)

Seed idea: *no music, no voice — the fabric speaks.* Feel-before-face register:
tactile, wordless, premium restraint. Borrows razee's ASMR engine without the
food/comedy. **Delivery = faceless, no VO.** Target ~15s micro. Solo-shootable.

**The whole reel lives or dies on audio.** Clip-on mic close to the action, dead
quiet room. If a sound is weak on playback, cut it — 4 great sounds beat 6 okay ones.

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | Audio (no VO, no music) | Note |
|------|--------|--------------------------|------|
| 0–2s | Steam iron gliding over cotton, steam rising in window light. | Iron hiss + steam burst. | Strongest sound first — this is the hook. |
| 2–5s | Kurti lifted onto a wooden hanger. | Hanger click on the rail. | One clean, crisp click. |
| 5–9s | Fingers flip through hanging kurtis, fabric brushing fabric. | Soft rustle, unhurried. | Slow — premium is pace. |
| 9–12s | A button pushed through its hole, close. | The tiny fabric-button pop. | Mic very close. |
| 12–15s | Dupatta lifted and let settle over the kurti; frame goes still. | The fall of fabric, then silence. | End on one second of stillness. |

**On-screen text (≤2 cards):**
- 0–2s: `sound on 🔈` (tiny, corner)
- 13–15s: `@saubhya.np` (small, quiet end frame)

## Shot list (solo, one phone, window light)

1. Iron + steam pass (phone propped at fabric level, mic clipped near the board).
2. Hanger click (re-record the sound separately if the take is weak).
3. Rail flip-through, slow.
4. Button close-up (phone on a stack of books, macro distance).
5. Dupatta drop + hold the still frame 2s.

Shoot each 2–3 times; pick the take with the best *sound*, not the best visual.

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** steam + hiss is a sensory pattern-interrupt; `sound on` flags the format instantly. Motion in frame from 0.0s.
- **Shareable line:** there isn't a line — the *format* is the share ("listen to this"). ASMR forwards on sensation.
- **Comment driver:** "kun sound sabai bhanda satisfying?" — pick-one prompt, zero effort to answer, not a beg.
- **Cuts:** already minimal. If one sound is weak, run 4 beats / ~12s.

## Checklist before posting
- Listen with eyes closed: every sound crisp? Any hiss/room noise → re-record.
- Watch muted: still reads premium (light, pace, clean frames)?
- ≤16s; no price, no words needed, ≤2 text cards.
