## Shot List

*Solo, phone propped — ~8 min to film.*

**Setup:** Mirror + clean background. Phone propped, rear camera, lock focus/exposure, face a window. No mic (no talking).

- **Mode:** Solo (phone propped)
- **Frame:** Waist-up, wardrobe / mirror in view

- **Action:** Grabs the kurti fast, throws it on in one quick motion — speed is the gag
- **Audio:** Ambient only
- **On-screen:** "kati ramro plan gareko outfit" — ma: 30 second ma uthayeko 😏
- **Note:** Make the grab genuinely quick — the contrast with how good it looks is the joke.

### Shot 2 — "full credit, no effort"
