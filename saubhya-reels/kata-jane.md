# Saubhya Reel — "Kata jane?"

Seed idea: *dress up at home and someone immediately asks "kata jane?"* — the
shared cultural truth that looking nice apparently needs a destination.
Relatable-culture-moment register — but **calm and observational**, not jump-cut
comedy (premium rule). **Delivery = faceless** with one optional soft face beat.
Target ~25s. Solo-shootable.

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–3s | **Faceless.** Slipping on a kurti at home, smoothing it in the mirror — clearly a normal afternoon. | "Ghar ma ali ramro lagayo bhane, ek jana le ta sodhi halcha — 'kata jane?'" | Wear something a little nice at home, and someone will definitely ask — "where are you going?" |
| 3–10s | Sitting down with a book / phone / chiya — visibly going nowhere. Small pause. | "Kahi jane haina. Just… lagauna man lagyo." | Going nowhere. Just… felt like wearing it. |
| 10–17s | Slow everyday beats: dupatta settling, adjusting a sleeve, light from the window. | "Hami lai yestai banaiyeko cha — ramro dekhinu paryo bhane kaaran chahincha re." | We've been wired like this — apparently looking nice needs a reason. |
| 17–25s | Calm end frame: sitting with chiya in the kurti, at home, content. (Face optional, soft.) | "Kaaran chahidaina. Ghar ma basera chiya khadai pani, ramro dekhina painchha." | No reason needed. Even sitting at home over tea, you're allowed to look good. |

**On-screen text (≤2 cards):**
- 0–3s: `"kata jane?"` (small, lower third)
- 17–25s: `kahi haina.` (small, clean)
