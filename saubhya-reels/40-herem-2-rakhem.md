# Saubhya Reel — "40 herem, 2 rakhem"

Seed idea: *handpicked is not a caption word — it's the kurtis you never see.*
Curator's-eye register (taste as content). **Delivery = faceless** — hands + fabric
on a table, window light, VO on top. Target ~30s. Solo-shootable on one phone.

⚠️ **Placeholders:** `[40]` / `[2]` and both rejection reasons must be HER real
numbers and real reasons. Never fabricate — if the real numbers are 12 and 3,
use 12 and 3.

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** mid-action hands + a number-pair hook ("[40] herem, [2] rakhem") — concrete, contrarian (a shop bragging about what it refused), opens one loop: *what got rejected and why?*
- **Shareable line:** "Handpicked bhaneko yehi ho — the [38] you never see."
- **Comment driver:** the caption question (fabric/print/stitching) — a real preference prompt, not a beg. Rejection reasons also naturally invite "show us the rejects."
- **Cuts:** drop reject #2 for a ~22s version; keep both if the reasons are genuinely different.

## Checklist before posting
- Watch muted: reads calm / premium? Rejects handled respectfully (no trash-talk of any brand — the flaw is the reason, unnamed)?
- ≤32s; hook numbers land in first 3s.
- Real numbers + real reasons only; no price, no backstory, no "fit" line, ≤2 text cards.
