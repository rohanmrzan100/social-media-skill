# Saubhya Reel — Monsoon Cotton

## Reel Goal

Create a soft seasonal Reel for Asar/monsoon. The feeling should be instantly relatable: rain outside, humidity inside, and the daily confusion of what to wear.

The kurti is the soft answer at the end, not the main pitch.

## What To Deliver In The Reel

Keep it calm, seasonal, and relatable.

The first line should feel like a real Asar problem, not a product intro.

Let the fabric appear as the natural solution near the end.

Only use light/easy/cotton claims if they are true for the exact kurti being shown.

Do not mention price, discount, or personal backstory.

## Simple Filming Direction

Show rain, window light, and the kurti fabric.

A few soft close shots are enough.

The main feeling should be: "yo mausam ma yesto fabric sajilo huncha."

## Quick Skill Check

Skip-rate defense: opens with a real seasonal problem, "Asar ma outfit choose garna sajilo hudaina."

Shareable line: "Bahira paani. Bhitra garmi."

Comment driver: "Asar ma tapai cotton prefer garnu huncha ki arko fabric?"

Product role: the kurti arrives as a soft solution, not a catalog pitch.
