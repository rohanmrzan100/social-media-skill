# Saubhya Reel — Yo Euta Detail

## Reel Goal

Create a short curator-style Reel about one small detail in a kurti. The reel should make people curious about the detail first, then quietly show why Saubhya picked the piece.

Main feeling: "Saubhya notices the small things that make a kurti feel different."

## Final Reel Script

Photo ma dherai dekhinna.

Tara real ma,

yo kurti ko euta detail le farak parcha.

[The detail].

Pahila herda sano kura jasto lagcha.

Tara lagauda thaha huncha.

Yo detail le neckline / sleeve / fall lai cleaner dekhaucha.

Overall look pani ali polished feel huncha.

Sometimes,

kurti ramro lagne reason big design hudaina.

Yestai sano detailing huncha.

Jun dekhda simple lagcha.

Tara overall look ma farak parcha.

Tei reason le yo piece Saubhya ma rakheko.

[Kurti name] from Saubhya.

Tapai yesto small details notice garnu huncha?

## What To Deliver In The Reel

Open with curiosity first: the detail is not obvious in photos.

Talk about one real detail only.

Do not make it generic.

The detail can be neckline, sleeve, piping, button, fall, lining, print placement, or fabric texture — but it has to be the actual reason this piece was picked.

Keep the tone soft and observant.

Do not mention price, discount, or personal backstory.

## Simple Filming Direction

Show the detail clearly first.

Then show the full kurti.

Keep it simple, close, and clean.

## Quick Skill Check

Skip-rate defense: opens with a curiosity gap, "Photo ma dherai dekhinna."

Shareable line: "Sometimes, kurti ramro lagne reason big design hudaina."

Comment driver: "Tapai yesto small details notice garnu huncha?"

Product role: the kurti proves Saubhya's taste through one specific detail, not a catalog pitch.
