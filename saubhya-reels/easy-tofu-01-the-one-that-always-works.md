## "The one that always works"

**Template:** Workhorse #1 — one relatable line + simple action over trending audio. Near-zero production. Her room, phone propped, ~12 seconds. Top-of-funnel (reach / binge-bank layer).

> **Note:** The specific line is hers to tweak — this is a concrete starting point, not a finished mandate. No prices, no backstory. Kurti = vehicle, felt not sold.

### The Moment (relatable hook)

The universal "I have to leave the house and I cannot be bothered to think about what to wear" feeling. The kurti shows up as the thing that always saves you. A woman NOT shopping for a kurti still watches this and thinks "this is me."

### On-Screen Text

- **0–2s (hook):** POV: ghar bata niskinu cha... tara kei pani lagaune mann chaina 🙃
- **Mid (turn):** so I just grab the one that never lets me down 🤝
- **End (close):** throw it on, look decent, Ready to face the world 🚶‍♀️ *(small saubhya logo, bottom corner)*

## Shot List

*Solo, phone propped — ~10 min to film.*

**Setup:** Her room or anywhere with a mirror + clean-ish background. Phone propped on a shelf/stack of books, rear camera, lock focus + exposure on her, face a window for light. No mic needed (no talking).

### Shot 1 — "can't be bothered"

- **Mode:** Solo (phone propped)
- **Frame:** Waist-up, phone at chest height, slight angle
- **Location:** In front of her wardrobe / mirror, tidy background
- **Action:** Looks at her open closet, mildly done-with-it — a small "ugh" beat, maybe one half-hearted hanger flick
- **Audio:** Ambient only (music added in edit)
- **On-screen:** POV: ghar bata niskinu cha... tara kei pani lagaune mann chaina 🙃
- **Note:** The expression sells it — bored/over-it, not acting hard. One natural take.

### Shot 2 — "the one that always works"

- **Mode:** Solo (phone propped)
- **Frame:** Waist-up → quick mirror check
- **Location:** Same spot, facing the mirror
- **Action:** Reaches straight for the kurti, throws it on, one quick satisfied mirror glance, turns to go
- **Audio:** Ambient only
- **On-screen:** so I just grab the one that never lets me down 🤝 → throw it on, look decent, baahira 🚶‍♀️
- **Note:** Keep it fast and real — the "done, sorted" energy is the payoff. Tag @saubhya.np in corner.

## Shoot-Day Checklist

- Battery + storage
- Lock focus/exposure before each take
- Face the window (sun behind the phone, not behind her)
- Clean the background
- Do one 5-second test record
- Then 2–3 takes of each shot
- Edit in CapCut: trim to ~12s, drop the trending audio, burn in the three text lines on the timing above
