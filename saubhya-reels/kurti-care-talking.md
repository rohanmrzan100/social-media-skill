# Saubhya Reel — Kurti Care

## Reel Goal

Create a calm, helpful talking Reel about why kurti colour fades and how to take care of it. The reel should feel natural, useful, and trustworthy — not like a list of instructions.

Main feeling: "Oh, this is actually useful. I should save this before washing my kurti."

## What To Deliver In The Reel

Talk calmly, like you are giving a useful tip to a friend.

The script should not sound like a numbered tutorial. Keep the lines short, with small pauses, so the rhythm stays alive.

Hold one kurti while talking so the reel feels real and connected to the topic.

Keep the tone helpful, not strict. The message is not "you are doing it wrong." The message is "yo garna sakincha, kurti ramro tikcha."

Do not mention price, discount, or personal backstory.

## Simple Filming Direction

One clean talking video is enough. Sit or stand in good natural light, hold the kurti, and speak directly to the camera.

You can add small close shots of the kurti being turned inside out or kept in shade, but only if it feels easy. The main deliverable is the talking reel.
