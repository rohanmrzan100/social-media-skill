# Saubhya Reel — "Bihana ko 20 minute"

Seed idea: *the 20 quiet morning minutes that are yours alone.* Unhurried-morning
register: aspirational calm, soft life — no hustle, no comedy. **Delivery =
faceless** (hands, chiya, light, wardrobe), VO on top. Target ~30s. Solo-shootable;
best shot in real morning light (6:30–8am).

---

## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–3s | Curtain pulled open, morning light floods in; steam off a chiya cup. | "Bihana ko yo 20 minute — yo mero matra time ho." | These 20 minutes in the morning — this time is mine alone. |
| 3–10s | Chiya poured, slow. Window, quiet street or plants outside. | "Ghar uthnu bhanda agadi. Phone ko duniya khulnu bhanda agadi." | Before the house wakes up. Before the phone's world opens. |
| 10–18s | Wardrobe opens; hand moves slowly across hanging kurtis. | "Chiya, ali kati shanti — ani aaja k lagaune bhanne sano decision." | Tea, a little peace — and the small decision of what to wear today. |
| 18–25s | The chosen kurti laid on the bed / held up to the light. | "Kasaile bhancha yo sano kura ho. Tara aafno din aafai set garnu — tyo sano kura hoina." | Some say it's a small thing. But setting your own day yourself — that's not small. |
| 25–30s | Final beat: kurti on (mirror glance, face optional) or ready on the hanger; chiya cup beside. Still frame. | "Aaja ko choice — [kurti name]." | Today's choice — [kurti name]. |

**On-screen text (≤2 cards):**
- 0–3s: `bihana ko 20 minute` (small, lower third)
- 25–30s: `[kurti name]` (small, clean)

## Shot list (solo, one phone, real morning light — shoot in one morning)

1. Curtain open (phone propped facing the window, step in from the side — hands only).
2. Chiya pour + steam close-up (backlit by the window for visible steam).
3. Wardrobe: slow hand pass across kurtis (phone propped inside/beside wardrobe).
4. Kurti laid on the bed / held to the light.
5. End frame: kurti on with a mirror glance (face optional) OR kurti + chiya cup still life.

Record VO separately in a quiet room; keep delivery slow — this reel should feel
like it breathes.

## Self-audit (instagram-unskip)

- **Skip defense (0–1s):** light flooding in + steam = motion and warmth in frame 1; "mero matra time" opens the identity loop (whose morning is this? mine too).
- **Shareable line:** "Aafno din aafai set garnu — tyo sano kura hoina."
- **Comment driver:** "tapai ko 'mero matra time' kun ho?" — everyone has an answer; it's about *her* morning, not the product.
- **Cuts:** merge beats 3–4 for a ~24s version.

## Checklist before posting
- Watch muted: reads calm, warm, aspirational-but-attainable (no luxury-flex props)?
- ≤32s; no price, no backstory, no "fit" line, ≤2 text cards.
- Kurti present but not pitched — it's the *choice*, not the subject.
