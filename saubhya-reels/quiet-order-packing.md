## THE SCRIPT

| Time | Visual | VO — Nepali (Romanized) | English |
|------|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| 0–3s | **Mid-action.** Hands folding a kurti on a clean table, wrapping paper ready. | "Order pack garda ma ali dhilo garchu. Jani jani." | I pack orders slowly. On purpose. |
| 3–10s | Careful fold, smoothing the fabric one last time. | "Kinabhane yo mero lagi yo kurti dekhne last choti ho. Tespachi yo kasai ko favourite banna jancha." | Because this is the last time I'll see this kurti. After this, it leaves to become someone's favourite. |
| 10–18s | Wrapping; tying/taping; unhurried. | "Fold garda sochchu — kasle lagaucha hola? Office jane din lagaucha, ki bihana ko chiya sanga?" | While folding I wonder — who'll wear it? On an office day, or with her morning tea? |
| 18–25s | Writing the name/city on the package, close-up of the pen. | "Naam lekhne yo moment chai kahilei purano hudaina. [City placeholder — real order] samma jadai cha yo." | Writing the name never gets old. This one's on its way to [city]. |
| 25–30s | The finished parcel, neat, by the window light. Still frame. | "Yo sano feeling ko lagi nai yo kaam garna man lagcha." | This small feeling is why I love doing this. |
