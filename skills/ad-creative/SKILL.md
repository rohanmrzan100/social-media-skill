# Ad Creative — paid Meta ads for @saubhya.np

> This is the **paid** skill. The organic skills (`saubhya`, `instagram-unskip`) treat the page as "a person who happens to sell kurtis" — *felt, not sold*, no prices. **An ad is the opposite job: its purpose is to sell.** Prices, offers, and a hard CTA are not just allowed here, they're required. This skill keeps Saubhya's *soul* (relatability, real voice, "this is me") so the ad doesn't read like a corporate commercial — but it is unapologetically built to convert spend into revenue.

It resolves the `ad-creative` reference that `instagram-unskip` already points to for paid IG ads.

**Scope: creative only.** Hooks, angles, scripts, on-screen text, ad copy, CTA, and the shot list. NOT campaign structure, budgets, bidding, pixel, or audience/targeting setup → that's **`meta-ads-strategy`**, the paired media-buying skill that briefs this one.

## The mode shift — what flips vs. organic (read first)

| | Organic (`saubhya`) | Paid ad (this skill) |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | watch time, relatability, reach | **clicks → purchases** (ROAS) |
| The kurti | vehicle, soft resolution at the end | **the subject** — show it, sell it |
| Prices / offers | ⛔ never | ✅ **in scope** (confirmed by her — see guardrail) |
| CTA | comment-driver question | **hard CTA** → app.reffero.com/saubhya |
| Catalog feel | avoid (caps reach) | fine *if* it converts — but story-led usually wins |

**What still holds (never flips):**
- **⛔ Backstory is still off-limits** — degree, origin, the wholesaler scam, hard early days. Private context only, in ads too.
- **Voice = code-switched Nepali-English (Romanized)**, warm and real. Never fake-salesy, never over-polished. Founder-led authenticity is the *advantage*, not a fallback.
- **The "this is me" test still matters** — the best ad still feels like a real woman's moment, not an infomercial.
- **She leads.** Bring a few angle hypotheses to test *with* her — not a giant concept menu she has to reject. Confirm before producing.

## Step 0 — load context + research (always, first)

1. Read **`saubhya-brand-context.md`** at repo root — positioning, audience, voice, benchmarks. Source of truth; if missing, ask, don't invent.
2. Mine **`saubhya-data/`** (her + 3 reference accounts' Apify scrapes) for the *research* this skill turns into angles: `latestComments` (real pains/objections in customers' own words), top captions, what reached. This is the same corpus `hook-machine` uses — point it at ad angles.
3. Her destination/offer: link is **app.reffero.com/saubhya**. Product line: short kurtis, kurti tops, 3-piece sets. Ships across Nepal (+ UK/Aus diaspora).

## Part A — Research into angle (the 6 pillars)

Before writing a single creative, map the research so it doesn't stay stuck in a vacuum. For each angle, fill all six:

- **Desire** — the outcome she actually wants. For Saubhya: *feel worthy / comfortable / look good without trying*; stop settling for kurtis that never fit.
- **Mechanism** — *how* the product delivers it. Saubhya's mechanism = **handpicked curation + the fit** (and fabric quality). This is the "why us" — not generic "we sell kurtis."
- **Authority** — why believe it. Real customer comments/DMs (the **real-comment overlay** — see Anatomy step 2), reviews, "ships across Nepal," repeat buyers. Pull these from `saubhya-data` `latestComments`.
- **Awareness level** — most of her market is **problem-aware** (kurtis fit badly / hard to find good ones) but **not solution-aware** about Saubhya. Cold audiences need the *problem* framed first; warm/retargeting audiences can go straight to product + offer. Match the angle to the awareness.
- **Avatar** — who exactly this creative speaks to (see sub-avatars below). One avatar per creative.
- **Angle** — the specific hook that ties *this avatar's* desire to the mechanism. The deliverable of the mapping.

> The #1 failure (Spence): research → blank doc → ad reverts to listing features. Don't list what the kurti *is*; sell what it *does* for one specific woman.

### Sub-avatars for Saubhya (a testing bank — confirm with her, don't dump)

Don't run one generic "buy our kurtis" ad. Split the market and tailor the angle. Candidates:

1. **"Nothing ever fits right."** Body-conscious; blames her body, not the cut. *Angle:* handpicked so it actually fits — write fresh wording each time. ⚠️ The old line *"it's not your body, it's the fit"* is **RETIRED** (Rohan flagged recycling it as cringe) — do not paid-ify it or paraphrase it.
2. **The daily-wear woman.** Wants to look put-together for work/college without effort. *Angle:* one kurti, instantly sorted, every day.
3. **The comfort-over-trends woman.** Tired of stiff, uncomfortable "trendy" pieces. *Angle:* comfortable *and* looks good — you don't have to choose.
4. **The occasion/festive buyer.** Needs something for an event, fast. *Angle:* handpicked sets, ships across Nepal in time.
5. **The diaspora (UK/Aus) Nepali.** Misses authentic, well-fitting kurtis from home. *Angle:* home, delivered — handpicked Nepali kurtis to your door.

## Part B — The psychology lens: valence + intensity

Every creative must strike an emotional chord hard enough to stop the scroll, then justify it with fact.

- **Valence** = positive or negative. Pick deliberately: **negative-high** (agitate a real pain — "every kurti you own fits wrong somewhere") or **positive-high** (aspirational relief — "the one that finally fits, the first time"). Winning ads sit at **high intensity** on one end, not lukewarm in the middle.
- **Facts as anchors.** Emotion grabs; **facts close.** After the emotional hook, drop undeniable, tangible facts to justify the buy: handpicked, the fit, fabric, ships across Nepal, the offer. Facts are where the research shines and trust is built.

## Part C — The winning-creative anatomy (7 steps)

Structure the creative in this order (Fraser). Each maps to Saubhya:

1. **Start in motion** — open on movement, not a static frame. Her turning, walking, reaching for the kurti. Earns the hook rate / scroll-stop in second one. (Same first-second discipline as `hook-machine`.)
2. **Real-comment graphic** — overlay a *genuine* customer comment/DM (familiar UI = trust + states the pain in the customer's own words). Source from `saubhya-data` `latestComments`. **Honesty rule: real comment, recreated cleanly — never fabricated.**
3. **Controversy / contrarian take** — a take that invites reaction: name a belief her buyers quietly hold, then overturn it. A proven move in her organic data; in paid it also drives the engagement Meta rewards. Belief/feeling only — never a swipe at the customer. ⚠️ Build a *fresh* belief→reframe each time — the retired "it's not your body, it's the fit" line (and paraphrases) is off-limits.
4. **Inspire the unsold** — answer the internal objection ("it won't fit me either" / "too expensive" / "looks cheap online"). Pivot from what it *is* to how it changes her day. This is where Part A's research pays off.
5. **Real / founder-led video** — gritty, authentic, phone-shot beats polished studio UGC. **This fits her production reality perfectly** — solo phone shoot is the *native, trust-building* format, not a compromise.
6. **Take advantage of trends** — wrap the locked message in a trending audio/format so it blends into feed and fights ad fatigue. (Her known reach lever — don't drop it as she did on the Lazy Day reel.)
7. **Buy-decision info + hard CTA** — remove friction: what it is, the offer, exactly what to do. CTA to **app.reffero.com/saubhya**. Unlike organic, this is direct and unambiguous.

## Part D — Craft: write in the environment

Do **not** draft ad copy in a blank doc — it comes out wordy and dies on a mobile screen. Write the on-screen text and copy **inside the actual creative layout** (Canva/Figma over the real frame). Forces it punchy, legible in the split-second of a scroll, and proves the hook fits the visual. (On-screen text rules from `instagram-unskip` still apply: ≤2 lines, 3–5 words, match to speech.)

## Guardrail — prices & claims must be confirmed, not invented

Prices/offers are in scope for ads, **but the exact numbers and any offer must come from Saubhya in writing** — never invent a price or discount. (Same discipline that bit the DGO paid reel: pricing must be confirmed by the brand.) If you don't have a confirmed number, leave a clear `[CONFIRM PRICE/OFFER]` placeholder and flag it. Keep claims to facts she can stand behind (handpicked, fit, fabric, shipping) — no unverifiable superlatives.

## Creative testing is a system, not a guess

You don't scale by throwing visuals at the wall. Produce **several angle×avatar variants** (e.g. avatars 1–3 above, each with a high-negative and a high-positive valence cut), test, let the data pick winners, then iterate the winner (new hooks, trends, social proof). Bring the *hypotheses* to her, run them, read the result.

## Production handoff

Pass the chosen creative(s) to **`solo-reel-shoot`** for a phone-shootable shot list — she films solo (tripod) or with one friend, budget phone + clip-on mic, outdoor-lean. Founder-led authenticity (step 5) means this constraint is an asset.

## Output

1. The avatar + angle (the 6 pillars filled), confirmed with her.
2. The chosen valence/intensity pitch.
3. The full ad creative: hook, beat-by-beat (the 7-step anatomy), on-screen text drafted in-layout, ad copy, and the hard CTA to app.reffero.com/saubhya — with any price/offer as a confirmed value or a flagged placeholder.
4. The `solo-reel-shoot` shot list.
5. If testing: the variant matrix (which avatars × valences to run).

## How it composes with the other skills

- **`meta-ads-strategy`** — the paired **media-buying** skill: objective, campaign/ad-set structure, placements, audience, budget, tracking, learning phase. It owns everything this skill marks out of scope, and it briefs this skill with the objective + the one action the ad must drive. Set the campaign there first, make the creative here.
- **`saubhya` / `instagram-unskip`** — the **organic** counterparts. This is what their "defer to ad-creative for paid" pointer means. Do not mix modes: organic = felt-not-sold, no prices; this = sell, with prices.
- **`hook-machine`** — same `saubhya-data` corpus; use it to sharpen the ad's hook line and to source the real-comment overlay. Frame 6 (Contrarian/Myth-Bust) is anatomy step 3.
- **`solo-reel-shoot`** — production layer; founder-led UGC is the goal, not a limitation.
- **`saubhya-brand-context.md`** — source of truth for voice, audience, and the off-limits backstory rule (which holds in paid too).

## One caution

The two source videos (Fraser Cottrell; Spence Pawliw) are **e-commerce DTC ad playbooks** — their exact metrics (44.9% hook rate, $200k spend, GLP-1/cod examples) are *their* cases, not laws. The durable, transferable ideas are: **research → specific avatar → angle**, **valence+intensity anchored by facts**, the **7-step creative anatomy**, **write-in-the-environment**, and **systematic testing**. Lead with those, adapt to Saubhya, ignore the rest.
